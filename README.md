# Sverve

Survival isn't hard enough.

Sverve adds thirst, heat, cold, wetness, and dry air to Minecraft survival, using familiar items and mechanics to make the world harder to live in.

- Drink water bottles to stay hydrated.
- Prepare for the climate. Heat increases hunger and thirst, cold slows healing, and extreme temperatures can kill.
- Stay dry or use water to cool off. Rain and swimming affect your body temperature.
- Warm up near campfires, torches, and lava, and protect yourself with Insulation, Heat Protection, or Thermal Protection enchantments.

Available for **Fabric** and **NeoForge**. Requires **Easy Config** and, on Fabric, **Fabric API**.

Native **Heavy Inventories** API integration.
